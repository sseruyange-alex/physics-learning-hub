How to Use

1
Fix resistance at 100 Ω and increase voltage — watch particles accelerate.
2
Fix voltage and raise resistance — observe particles slow down.
3
Read current from the green readout — this is V ÷ R (Ohm's Law).
4
Record readings, then plot a graph to verify linearity.

Experiment 2 — Simple Pendulum

Period formula: T = 2π√(L/g). Adjust length and calculate g experimentally.

How to Perform

1
Set pendulum length using the slider — animation updates immediately.
2
Click ▶ Start to begin swinging; ⏸ Stop to pause.
3
Physically time 20 complete swings, divide by 20 to find T.
4
Enter your measured T in the second slider to compute g = 4π²L / T².
